EXPERIMENT & ANALYSIS

Relationship between compression and quality

Choose different latent dimensions and observe the expected trend. Smaller dimensions retain less information; larger dimensions can preserve more detail but require a larger latent representation.

Latent dimensionCompressionExpected reconstruction qualityTypical observation
4–8Very highLowFine details may disappear
12–32Moderate–highMediumOverall shape often remains
36–64ModerateGoodMore edges and textures retained
68–128LowerVery goodMore detail, less compression
↗
Key finding

Increasing latent-space dimension generally improves reconstruction quality, while reducing the compression ratio. The best dimension balances image quality and storage needs.

METHODOLOGY

How an autoencoder works

  1. Preprocess

    Resize images and normalize pixel values.

  2. Encode

    The encoder maps an image to a compact latent vector.

  3. Decode

    The decoder reconstructs an image from that vector.

  4. Evaluate

    Compare original and reconstructed images using MSE or PSNR.

EVALUATION

Useful metrics

MSE

Mean Squared Error measures the average squared pixel difference. Lower MSE usually means a closer reconstruction.

PSNR

Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio measures reconstruction quality in decibels. Higher PSNR usually indicates better quality.

Compression ratio

Original representation size divided by compressed representation size. A higher ratio means stronger compression.
